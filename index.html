Questions

What does it cost?

It depends on the route. Call me at 732-300-3835 with where the car is and where it's going, and I'll give you a flat rate for the trip.

Can I really pack the car?

Yes. That's the big difference from a trailer carrier. Load the trunk and back seat with what you're bringing for the season, and leave me room to drive.

Do you drive north in the spring too?

Yes. I drive cars down to Florida for the winter and back up to New Jersey for the summer.

When is a trailer carrier the better choice?

I'd rather you get the right service than the wrong one from me. A carrier makes more sense when:

  • the lowest price matters more than when the car arrives, and you can wait for an open spot on a truck;
  • the car doesn't run, so it can't be driven;
  • it's a collector car you'd rather not put miles on.

For everyone else, having your car driven down, packed, by one person is the easier way.
